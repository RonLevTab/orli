The problem

Optima runs the clinic.
It just doesn't talk to patients.

Optima's calendar was built for your staff, not for patients online. So they still phone the clinic or walk in to book, and the front desk types every appointment in by hand. Orli opens that same calendar to your website, without replacing anything you already run.

How it works

One booking engine, two front doors.

Every path ends the same way: a proposed slot, a patient confirmation, a real write to Optima, and a confirmation message. That's it.

01 / PICK

Patient picks an open time

The booking tool shows real openings from Optima, the same ones your staff see.

02 / VERIFY

Confirms with a phone code

A quick code sent to their phone confirms the patient. No passwords, no accounts to manage.

03 / WRITE

Orli books it in Optima

The appointment goes straight into your calendar. If anything goes wrong, it asks the patient to try again, so you never get a mistaken booking.

04 / CONFIRM

Everyone gets confirmation

The patient gets a confirmation and your calendar is already up to date. Reschedules and cancellations work the same way.

Trust & privacy

Built to keep patient details safe.

Orli handles patient information with care. Here's what that means for you, in plain terms.

🔐 Patient data stays private

Patient details are never exposed on your public website. Everything sensitive is handled securely behind the scenes.

📱 No accounts, no passwords

Patients confirm with a quick code sent to their phone. Nothing to sign up for, no password to forget, and it keeps out spam.

🚧 Never a double booking

If anything goes wrong, Orli asks the patient to try again rather than risk a mistaken or duplicated appointment.

🧾 Only what's needed

Orli asks for just enough to book the appointment, and follows Israeli healthcare privacy rules.

Frequently asked

A few things teams ask before getting started.

Do we have to replace Optima?Integration

No. Orli sits on top of the Optima calendar you already run. Nothing changes for your staff. The manual booking work simply disappears.

How long does it take to get started?Onboarding

Days, not months. We connect to your Optima, set up your services and doctors, and give you a simple snippet to add to your site.

Does the front desk lose control of the calendar?Control

Never. Patients only ever book into real, available slots your rules already allow. Staff keep working in Optima exactly as they do today.

Is patient data safe on a public website?Security

Yes. Patient details are never exposed on your public site. Every booking is confirmed by a code sent to the patient's phone, and saved for you.

What if two patients grab the same slot?Reliability

If two people reach for the same time, Orli notices and offers the next opening, so no one walks away thinking they're booked when they're not.
