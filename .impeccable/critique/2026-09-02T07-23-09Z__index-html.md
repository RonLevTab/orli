## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific in voice, generic in structure, and self-undermining in its centrepiece.** Assessment A's judgment, reached without seeing the previous review: the writing would survive a blind test; the demo would not.

**What is unmistakably this product.** The copy could not be lifted to another company — "הטלפון מצלצל שוב… מי שמתקשר אחרי חמש שומע תא קולי", then the objection-killer one line later: "אורלי לא מחליפה את המזכירה. היא משחררת אותה מהטלפון". Naming אופטימה directly throughout is the claim a generic scheduling SaaS cannot make. A also singled out two pieces of reasoning as design-director work: the `html[lang="he"]` block that drops synthetic italics and eases Latin tracking, and `.check-list--plain` swapping the teal ✓ for a neutral bullet on privacy.html because a check-mark reads as a *benefit* when the list is a *disclosure*.

## Overall Impression

Three phases of work moved this from 19 to 22, and the gains are real and durable — control, error prevention, error recovery, and recognition all improved a full point each, and every structural a11y defect from the first run is now measurably closed.

But the score barely moved because the two things that actually decide whether a clinic owner converts were never touched: **the demo still demonstrates a booking her clinic would refuse, and the form still dead-ends with no way to reach anyone.** Heuristic 2 even went *down* a point, because the fixes elsewhere threw the widget's unaddressed terminology drift into relief.

The biggest opportunity is unchanged from the first run and still unclaimed: the clinic owner never sees her own calendar.

## What's Working

1. **The positioning spine is written, not asserted.** Every section resolves to one falsifiable fact rather than a benefit adjective, which is exactly what a competitor cannot copy.
2. **The mobile scrollytelling substitution is a real design decision** — it abandons the *technique* below 920px while preserving the *payoff*, which is the thing most responsive rebuilds get backwards. Verified: tapping tab 5 advances both the step text and the widget scene.
3. **"Say only what's shipped" is now enforced visually, not just editorially** — `.rung.done` solid teal versus `.rung.next` dashed neutral plus a grey pill, paired with `.check-list--plain` refusing a teal ✓ for disclosure content. A team reasoning about what an icon *means in context*.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The only conversion path dead-ends, and is styled as a success
**What.** `FORM_ENDPOINT = ''`, so a correctly filled form returns "הטופס עדיין לא מחובר, ולכן הפרטים לא נשלחו." The `!FORM_ENDPOINT` branch calls `clearMarks()` and then never re-adds `is-error` — verified in source — so the failure paints in the same neutral pill as every ordinary note. And **zero** `mailto:`, `tel:`, or WhatsApp links exist across all four pages; the footer's "צור קשר" points back to `#contact`.
**Why it matters.** Every CTA on all four pages funnels here. The visitor does the work, is told it was wasted, is given no other channel — and the colour says it went fine. For an Israeli SMB where WhatsApp is the default business channel, this is disqualifying.
**Fix.** One line adds `is-error` and stops the mis-signal. Then a real fallback channel in the CTA band and footer. Then the endpoint.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The demo demonstrates a booking the buyer's clinic would refuse
**What.** Availability is hardcoded to days-of-month 3/5/8/12/19/26 with no weekday logic. Verified against today's date: in September 2026 that is **Thu, Sat, Tue, Sat, Sat, Sat** — four of six offered dates are Shabbat — and `DEMO_PREFILL` books **Saturday 5 September at 10:30**. Scene 2, whose job is to prove availability, offers exactly **one** slot. The booked object is called פגישה, then תור, then הזמנה across six steps. Every string renders twice, Hebrew plus English at `opacity:.5`.
**Why it matters.** PRODUCT.md makes demo fidelity a *marketing* requirement. A clinic owner asking "will this respect my schedule?" is shown a Shabbat appointment. That one glance costs more credibility than six well-written steps beside it earn — and it is a lottery that changes with the month you visit.
**Fix.** Generate availability from the current date skipping Saturdays; give scene 2 four or five slots; pick one noun (**פגישה**, matching all surrounding copy); suppress the English secondary line on this Hebrew-only site.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The hero cannot fit any viewport, and has no call to action
**What.** `.nav` is `position: sticky` and occupies 65px of flow; `.hero` is `min-height: 100dvh`. Independently verified: hero top at y=65, height equal to the full viewport, **bottom overflowing by exactly 65px** — the nav height, on every device. The hero markup contains an eyebrow, an `h1`, a lead, and an `aria-hidden` picture. Zero buttons.
**Why it matters.** Two comments in the stylesheet state that fitting the hero to one viewport was the goal. On a Persuade surface, the primary action has no presence in the primary screen.
**Fix.** `min-height: calc(100dvh - 65px)`, or make `.nav` fixed and pad `main`. Add a primary CTA plus a quiet "צפו בדמו" under the lead. Consider swapping the hero mockup from the *confirmation* screen to the *pick a time* screen — the moment the headline actually promises.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Almost nothing has a focus style
**What.** The whole codebase contains three focus rules (`.code-copy:focus-visible`, `.field input:focus`, `.obw-field input:focus`) — confirmed by count. Nav links, `#navToggle`, `.faq-btn`, `.scrolly-tab`, `.btn-dark`, `.btn-cream`, footer links and every widget option have none. `.btn-dark` also sets `overflow: hidden`, which clips the UA default ring on the primary CTA.
**Why it matters.** PRODUCT.md sets "WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operability" as the floor. A keyboard user tabbing to the main CTA sees a clipped ring or nothing. The project is failing its own written standard.
**Fix.** `:where(a, button, [tabindex]):focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--teal-deep); outline-offset: 2px; }` plus `.btn-dark:focus-visible { overflow: visible }`. About four lines.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] The CTA form still fails AA — the earlier fix improved it but did not clear the bar
**What.** The band is `linear-gradient(155deg, #0a5d5a, #0f8a86)`, not a flat colour, and the inputs are translucent white over it. Composited properly across the gradient: placeholder **4.21:1 → 2.73:1** (needs 4.5), border **3.51:1 → 2.31:1** (needs 3.0), and the **typed value itself 5.75:1 → 3.48:1** — solid white text that fails AA at the light end of the band.
**Why it matters.** This is the form the whole site funnels into, and the failure is worst at the bottom of the gradient where the inputs actually sit.
**Fix.** Stop relying on translucency over a gradient: give the inputs an opaque dark fill derived from `--teal-deep`, or flatten the band behind the form.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] Three RTL/CSS correctness bugs, two of them corrupting Hebrew
**What.** (a) `.faq-panel-inner p { padding: 0 22px 20px 76px }` is a *physical* shorthand in an RTL document — the 76px meant to clear the right-hand `.faq-plus` icon lands on the left, so above 640px the answer tucks under the icon and gains a dead gutter opposite. (b) All **5** `[להשלמה משפטית]` placeholders in privacy.html carry `direction:ltr` on pure Hebrew, reversing the words and mispairing the brackets — the attribute was correctly copied from integration.html's real LTR code tokens and wrongly applied to Hebrew. (c) An empty `@media (max-width: 900px) {}` block and a `/* comparisonn */` typo.
**Fix.** `padding-inline-start: 76px` · drop `direction:ltr` from the Hebrew placeholders and give them a real warning treatment · delete the empty block.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Minor Observations

- Fraunces + Inter load render-blocking on all four pages and **never render**; cutting to Heebo alone removes ~7 unused font resources from the LCP path.
- `index.html` ships `orli-wordmark.png` twice behind `display: none` — downloaded, never shown. The other three pages correctly omit it.
- **62 inline `style=` attributes** across four static pages, including `font-size:1.7rem` repeated five times — the type scale isn't expressible in the system, so it's hand-patched per instance.
- `.scrolly-tabs` uses `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`/`aria-selected` with no `role="tabpanel"` and no `aria-controls` — an incomplete ARIA pattern that announces worse than plain buttons.
- `.faq-btn` has `aria-expanded` but no `aria-controls`; the panel has no `id`.
- `#formNote` uses `role="status"` (polite) for validation *errors*; `role="alert"` would announce promptly.
- Widget scene 5 drops the "עב" language control every other scene shows — the chrome changes shape at the final step.
- `about.html` uses the teal-✓ `.check-list` for principles, contradicting the rule documented three lines away in the stylesheet.
- privacy.html carries no date and no "last updated", unusual even for a marked draft.
- Touch targets under 44×44 at 390px are widespread beyond the two fixed ones: footer links ~24px tall (14 on index) and the widget's calendar arrows at 13×20 and 9×20.

## Questions to Consider

1. **If the demo is the primary persuader, why does it demonstrate a booking your buyer's clinic would never accept?** What does it say that nobody caught this in a month where the dates happened to land on Shabbat?
2. **What would the page look like if the success screen were the CTA?** Scene 6 currently offers only "restart the demo" at the emotional peak.
3. **Who is the English in the widget for?** The site is Hebrew-only by permanent policy, so the most persuasive element is the one place contradicting principle 5. Is "faithful mirror" being read as *identical* when it should mean *equivalent*?
4. **The copy insists nothing changes for staff. Does the design say it?** The strongest reassurance on the page is a sentence. As a picture — the Optima calendar with an appointment simply appearing in it — it doesn't exist anywhere on the site.
5. **What does it mean that a product selling "stop making people call you" has no phone number?** The absence is currently the last thing a visitor experiences.
6. **If Fraunces never renders, is the brand a serif/sans pairing — or is it Heebo, and the spec hasn't caught up?**
