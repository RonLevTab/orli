## Overall Impression

The craft is real and the argument is right: the film and the demo carry the pitch, the copy is honest and specific. What holds the score down is one decision, the desktop scroll takeover, and the half-empty screens it forces the page to present one at a time. The single biggest opportunity is letting the visitor skim again and putting a door to the conversion at the demo's peak.

## What's Working

1. **The bridge film is the argument.** After-hours timestamp, a booking landing among other patients' real appointments, reduced-motion respected. The most product-specific element on the site, on screen two.
2. **Phone demo layout.** Six steps in a fixed list, current one open, passed ones ticked, tap to jump, widget zoomed so no step scrolls inside the card and the confirm button never clips.
3. **Copy discipline.** Objection-first FAQ, shipped vs "בקרוב" that survives a squint, no fabricated logos or metrics, Optima never a foil.
